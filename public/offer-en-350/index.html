What is the Photo Station?

Every guest deserves a special keepsake.

Our Photo Station creates black-and-white wedding portraits with you and your guests at the heart of the experience. It is an elegant, timeless alternative to a traditional photo booth.

During three hours of service, we take professional portraits against a white backdrop using studio lighting. We edit the photos, upload them to the cloud and print them on the spot, so everyone can leave your wedding with a keepsake in hand.

Step by step

How does the Photo Station work?

01

Two photographers

One photographer helps your guests pose and takes the portraits. The other edits and prints the photos and looks after the guestbook tablet. This keeps everything moving smoothly, without long queues.

02

Instant prints

The camera is connected to a computer. Within a few minutes, each finished portrait is edited, uploaded to the cloud and sent to the printer. Guests receive a full-size 10 × 15 cm print.

03

A guestbook to keep

Guests use a stylus to write their wishes on a tablet. After the wedding, we combine their messages and portraits into an elegant, minimalist guestbook, ready within 30 working days.

A gallery for your guests

Photos straight to your phone.

A QR code is available at the Photo Station. Scan it to open an elegant online gallery straight away — no app to install and no need to ask for a link.

We add portraits throughout the reception. Your guests can browse the gallery and download their favourite photos directly to their phones.

Included in both Essential and Exclusive.

Live access depends on the mobile signal and internet speed at your venue.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Does this replace group photos with the couple?

No. The Photo Station focuses on guests on their own, in pairs or in groups of three or four. Traditional group photos with the couple are usually taken by your main wedding photographer. Of course, the couple is welcome to use the Photo Station too.

Will it interfere with our wedding photographer?

Not at all. The Photo Station is an additional experience in a separate area, and does not take over your wedding photographer’s work. We photograph weddings ourselves, so we know how the day flows and how to work alongside your photographer.

Will we always get the same team of photographers?

Not necessarily. We have several Photo Stations and photographers, and often provide more than one service on the same day. Your team may vary depending on availability, date and location, but every booking meets the same standards of quality and professionalism.

How long does it take to receive the photos?

We edit portraits on site. From taking the photo to having the finished image in the online gallery and at the printer usually takes just a few minutes.

When is the best time to open the Photo Station?

We will work around your wedding schedule. In our experience, the best time is after the first round of dancing.

Do you travel to our area?

For our three-hour service, we generally travel to venues within about three hours’ driving time from Gliwice — roughly 300 km. This includes Upper Silesia as well as Wrocław, Opole, Częstochowa, Kraków and Warsaw. Please contact us to confirm your venue and any longer-distance arrangements.

How much space do you need?

We need a clear area of at least 4 × 4 metres to work comfortably. Please check with your venue in advance. Venues sometimes mistake us for a traditional photo booth, but our studio setup needs a little more space.
